# Admin guide (for the HaTikvah Care team)

Sign in at `/admin`.

**Add or change prices**
* Products: type the price in the ₹ box and press Enter. Add a unit (per piece, per pack…) in the next box.
* Many at once: Products, *Export CSV*, open in Excel, fill `price_rupees` (and `price_unit`, `min_order`), save as CSV, then *Import CSV*, *Preview*, *Apply changes*.
* Products without a price show **Price on request** on the website. The owner can hide all prices site-wide in *Company & prices*.

**Add a product:** Products, *+ Add product*. Choose the category, add photos (JPG/PNG/WebP up to 2 MB), options (like Shampoo, Conditioner…), and tick *Show on website*. Untick it to hide a product without deleting it.

**Home page:** tick the ★ on a product to feature it on the home page. Category order is set in *Categories*.

**Enquiries:** every quote request and contact message lands in *Enquiries*. Change the status (new, contacted, quoted, won, closed), add notes, export to CSV.

**Team:** owners can add staff. Staff manage products, categories and enquiries; only owners see company settings, team and the activity log.

**Activity log:** shows who changed what and when, including old and new prices.
